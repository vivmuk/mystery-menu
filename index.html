MysteryMenu

Two chefs. Five ingredients each. Nobody sees them until the referee says the word.

Bid for every ingredient against the clock, plate the dish, then let the table vote on the one they would actually eat.

01

Scan in

The host opens a room, the table scans the barcode. No accounts, no passwords.

02

Bid blind

Ten ingredients, ten coins, a ten second clock. Say “take it” and the ingredient is yours.

03

Vote

The table picks the dish they would rather eat. Best of three rounds takes the match.
